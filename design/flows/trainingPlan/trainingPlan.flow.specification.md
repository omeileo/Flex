# Training Plan Flow — Screen Specifications

Runna-inspired. Mobile: 390×844.

## Step 1: Multi-week plan overview

- Header: "Your Plan" + calendar icon
- Stacked week cards (Week 1 bordered = current)
- Each card: date range, workout count, total volume
- Workout rows with modality color dots
- Tab bar: Today | **Plan** | Coach | Profile

## Step 2: Week overview sheet

- Bottom sheet over plan screen
- Title: "Week 1 Overview"
- Stats: Total workouts, volume
- Workout cards with left gradient bar
- CTA: View full week

## Step 3: Today daily workout

- Header: Week 2/8 dropdown + progress ring
- **Location chip** (new): active gym name, e.g. "Home Gym ▾" — tap opens location switcher or settings
- Week strip: Mon–Sun, today highlighted (black circle)
- Section: "Today's workouts"
- Workout cards: title, duration, modality bar, checkbox
- Coach note row (optional)
- Primary CTA: Start workout
- **Equipment mismatch sheet** (branch): if session needs equipment missing at active location — Switch location · Edit equipment · Continue anyway
- Tab bar: **Today** active

## Step 4: Weekly progression

- Header: Plan Overview + week chevrons
- Segmented progress bar for week
- Coach message card
- Workout list for selected week
- CTA: Go to current week

## Step 5: AI plan adjustment

- Modal/bottom sheet
- Icon: refresh/sparkle
- Headline: "Adjust your plan?"
- Copy: "You missed 2 sessions. Flex can readapt your remaining weeks."
- Actions: Readapt plan (primary) · Keep original (secondary)
